# Product roadmap

This is a proposed sequence, not a committed delivery schedule.

## 1. Establish a trustworthy read-only product surface

Validate source-to-fact parity with design partners, define access controls and
retention, and provide operators with workflow, time-window, quality, and
cohort views backed by explainable records.

## 2. Make evaluation operational

Define supported experiment assignment guarantees, plan ownership, fixed
stopping, policy review, and operator review. Only then consider scheduling
the existing evaluator or integrating traffic allocation.

## 3. Operationalize governed changes

Design production identity and approver roles, notifications, policy
configuration, approval experience, and reviewable change history. Preserve
the current rule that evaluation never substitutes for authorization.

## 4. Expand carefully

Evaluate additional channels and optimization areas only after API contracts,
measurement validity, customer demand, and failure/recovery requirements are
understood.

Each step depends on customer discovery and production-readiness gates. No
delivery date or customer commitment is implied.
