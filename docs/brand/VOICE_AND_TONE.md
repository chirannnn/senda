# Voice and tone

Senda communicates with clarity, restraint, and operational precision.

- Prefer measured statements to superlatives.
- Name the evidence and scope behind a claim.
- Use plain language for users and precise terminology for engineering detail.
- State uncertainty and missing controls directly.
- Explain what a system will do when a request is ambiguous or data is
  insufficient.
- Describe automation as bounded by policy and approval.
- Do not borrow urgency, customer quotes, market statistics, or traction that
  has not been verified.
