# Senda brand identity

## Name and line

**Senda**  
**Make every interaction count.**

The name is used for the product direction described in this repository. No
trademark, domain, or company-name availability review is represented here.

## Character

Senda should feel clear, dependable, intelligent, restrained, and technically
serious. It should sound like an operator's trusted decision system: precise
about evidence, transparent about uncertainty, and calm when a workflow needs
human review.

## Visual direction

Use a minimal typographic identity with generous whitespace and a neutral
palette. Prefer diagrams that explain data flow and decision boundaries over
decorative graphics. Do not imply a finished dashboard, live customer system,
or autonomous agent through fabricated screenshots or illustrations.

## Identity rules

- Describe Senda as an operations and optimization platform direction, not as
  a dialer, voice bot, analytics-only dashboard, or AI wrapper.
- State whether a capability is in the current prototype or on the roadmap.
- Never imply customers, deployment, availability, results, or traction that
  this repository does not establish.
- Keep external product, company, and social links out until the owner verifies
  and supplies them.
