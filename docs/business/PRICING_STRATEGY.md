# Pricing strategy questions

Pricing is not established. Before choosing a model, research:

- Whether customers buy by workflow, interaction volume, team, or measured
  outcome.
- Predictable cost requirements for event retention and external model or
  validation calls.
- Procurement, security, and integration costs.
- How pricing interacts with customer-approved experimentation and governance.
- Whether customers expect pilot pricing, annual commitments, or usage tiers.

Do not publish a price list until interviews and delivery-cost analysis support
one. No existing customer, revenue, or willingness-to-pay claim is made.
