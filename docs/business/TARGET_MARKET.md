# Target market hypothesis

## Initial profile to validate

The initial ideal-customer-profile hypothesis is a mid-sized or large
organization with recurring outbound engagement programs, several workflow
configurations, material compliance oversight, and a data/platform team able
to integrate an operational API.

Possible first verticals include financial services and insurance because the
prototype's local domain fixtures model renewal and follow-up workflows. Those
fixtures are not evidence of customer demand, market size, or product-market
fit.

## Likely buying and operating roles

- Operations leader: outcome ownership and workflow efficiency.
- Compliance/risk: policy review and change traceability.
- Platform/data engineering: integrations, storage, and recovery.
- Workflow supervisor: local configuration and approval context.

## Discovery questions

Validate workflow volume and current tools, metric definitions, approval
ownership, integration constraints, privacy/retention requirements, cost of
bad changes, and the evidence needed to approve an automated recommendation.
Do not present this profile as a selected market until interviews support it.
