# Business model hypotheses

No pricing, revenue, customer contracts, or willingness-to-pay research is
present in the repository. Possible models to test after customer discovery:

- Annual platform subscription tied to deployed workflows or operating teams.
- Usage-based pricing tied to observed interactions or evaluated decisions.
- A base platform fee plus volume tiers, with explicit cost controls for
  data retention and external model/validation usage.

The trade-off is predictability versus alignment to customer value and
variable infrastructure cost. Any model must account for integration,
storage, support, governance, and model/validation expenses. These are
options for discovery, not an announced price or business model.
