# Alternatives and differentiation hypotheses

There is no competitive research in this repository. These are categories a
buyer may compare, not a validated market map:

- Native reporting and optimization in an existing contact-center platform.
- Internal analytics built on call events and a warehouse.
- Manual review by operations, compliance, and data teams.
- Specialized campaign-management or conversational-automation products.

The product hypothesis is that a dedicated evidence-to-change layer could
connect performance measurement, controlled evaluation, approval, safe
application, and post-change audit across workflow systems. This hypothesis
must be compared against actual buying alternatives, integration effort,
decision quality, and switching costs. No competitor superiority or unique
market position is claimed.
