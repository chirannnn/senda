# Go-to-market hypothesis

There is no launch, sales pipeline, partner network, or customer traction
established by this repository. A cautious initial approach to validate would
be founder-led discovery with a small number of design partners that have a
clear outbound operations owner and can provide a documented integration
contract.

Potential sequence:

1. Interview operations, compliance, and platform owners about current
   measurement and approval workflows.
2. Select one bounded workflow and agree on outcome definitions, data access,
   retention, safety policy, and success criteria before integration.
3. Run in read-only observation mode first; compare Senda-derived facts with
   the source system.
4. Introduce recommendations only after metric parity and audit review.
5. Enable approved changes only after identity, authorization, recovery, and
   operational controls are established.

Distribution channels, partner strategy, and sales cycle remain unknown.
This document is a validation plan, not evidence that these steps have
occurred.
